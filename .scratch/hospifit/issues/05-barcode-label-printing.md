# 05 — Barcode label printing

**What to build:** Office Staff can print physical barcode labels for assets — one at a
time, or many at once laid out to fit a chosen paper size — directly from the browser, to
affix to the client's equipment.

**Blocked by:** 04 — Asset registry & barcode generation.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Office Staff can print a single asset's barcode label from that asset's detail view.
- [ ] Office Staff can select multiple assets and print them as one batch.
- [ ] Bulk labels are laid out in a grid sized to a configurable target paper size (no
      dedicated label-printer driver/hardware integration required).
- [ ] Printing works via the browser's native print flow (print-to-PDF or any printer),
      with no external service dependency.
